Not just Ram Ram Ram Ram — a sankalpa to set, a day to reflect on, and a letter to Ram, alongside every jaap.

The old way was repetition — writing His name again and again, page after page. Smaran keeps the devotion, but gives it somewhere to go: a goal for the day, a moment of honest reflection, and a letter to Ram, in your own words.
